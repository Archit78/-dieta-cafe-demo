What Dieta Café is

A cloud‑kitchen for people who want food that tastes good and keeps score — every rice bowl, salad, sandwich, and shake comes with its calories, protein, fat and carbs printed right next to the price.

Order from the full menu whenever you like, or subscribe to a weekly or monthly plan and let a rotation of rice bowls, salads, sandwiches and more arrive at your door — delivery included. Based in Ankleshwar, Dieta Café keeps the format simple: fresh ingredients, honest nutrition numbers, and packages built around how you actually eat through the week.

About Dieta Café

Your goals, our nutrition.

Dieta Café is a healthy-food cloud kitchen built around a simple idea: nutrition that feels right, not restrictive. Every dish is served with its full nutrition breakdown, from rice bowls to shakes, so what you eat is always a clear choice rather than a guess.

The kitchen is based in Ankleshwar, working off a rotating menu of rice bowls, salads, sandwiches, pizza, burgers, shakes, oats and desserts — available à la carte or through a weekly and monthly subscription.
